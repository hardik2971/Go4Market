# Go4Market Partner app: Play Store listing

**App name** (max 30 chars)
Go4Market Partner

**Short description** (max 80 chars)
Sell or deliver with Go4Market: manage orders, products, deliveries & earnings.

**Full description** (max 4000 chars)

Go4Market Partner is the app for the sellers and delivery partners who power the Go4Market marketplace. Choose your role when you sign up: Sell on Go4Market or Deliver with Go4Market.

**For sellers**
• Quick store onboarding: store details, location pin, GST, bank details, logo and banner
• Live dashboard with today's orders, revenue, sales chart and low-stock alerts
• New-order alerts; accept, pack and hand over in a few taps
• Assign your own delivery partners, or let Go4Market find the nearest rider
• Self-pickup order handling with secure pickup codes
• Add products from your phone: photos, variants, price and MRP, stock, veg/non-veg and custom fields
• Barcode scanning for products and fast billing
• AI-assisted product descriptions
• Built-in POS for walk-in customers, with cash, card or UPI and shareable receipts
• Stock management with quick restock
• Store coupons, reviews and return handling
• Earnings, transactions and withdrawal requests

**For delivery partners**
• Go online or offline with one tap
• See available deliveries near you and accept the ones you want
• Map with store and customer locations, and one-tap navigation in Google Maps
• Call the store or the customer from the app
• Swipe to confirm each step: picked up, out for delivery, delivered
• Secure delivery with customer OTP and cash-on-delivery confirmation
• Quick-delivery countdown so you never miss a promise
• Delivery history, daily earnings, cash in hand and withdrawals

**Location use:** delivery partners share their live location only while they are Online, so customers can track their order and nearby deliveries can be assigned. Going Offline stops tracking.

Available in English and Hindi, with light and dark themes.
