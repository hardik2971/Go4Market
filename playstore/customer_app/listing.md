**App name** (max 30 chars)
Go4Market: Grocery, Food & More

**Short description** (max 80 chars)

**Full description** (max 4000 chars)

Go4Market brings your neighbourhood's best stores to one app. Order fresh groceries, hot food, medicines and everyday shopping from trusted local sellers, and get them at your door in as little as 15 minutes.

**Everything in one app**
• Grocery: fruits, vegetables, dairy, staples and household essentials
• Food: meals and snacks from local kitchens and restaurants, with veg/non-veg marks and add-ons
• Pharmacy: medicines, wellness and personal care
• Shopping: electronics, fashion, home and kitchen

**Fast, flexible delivery**
• Quick delivery in 15 or 30 minutes, with a live arrival countdown
• Schedule a delivery slot that suits you, or pick up from the store yourself
• Track your rider live on the map, from the store to your door
• Share a one-time delivery code with your rider for a safe handover

**Shop smarter**
• Smart search, plus voice search for several items at once ("milk, bread and eggs")
• Filters, sorting and grid or list views
• Similar-product suggestions when something is out of stock
• Ratings and reviews from real customers
• Recently viewed items and personalised picks

**Save more**
• Coupons and daily offers
• Go4Market wallet for refunds, cashback and quick checkout, with partial wallet payment
• Refer friends and both of you earn wallet credit
• Membership plans with free delivery and extra discounts
• Product subscriptions for daily needs like milk and bread

**Pay your way**
UPI, cards and net banking (Razorpay), international cards (Stripe), PayPal, wallet or cash on delivery.

**Easy after-sales**
Order history, one-tap reorder, simple returns with refunds to your wallet, and order updates by push notification.

Go4Market supports English and Hindi, with light and dark themes.

Questions? Write to support@go4market.com.
